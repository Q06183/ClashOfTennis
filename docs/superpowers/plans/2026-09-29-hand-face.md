# Three hand/face prototypes — implementation plan

> Execute inline with test-driven checkpoints; no agents or paid generation.

**Goal:** Reference-informed male/female and left/right hand prototypes for Lin,
Mei and Noah, without replacing the seven other characters.

**Architecture:** Keep the ten 21-joint GLBs as immutable comparison inputs.
Export three locally refined GLBs under `public/models/prototypes/`, tagged
`handFaceRevision=1`. Only these activate a wrist/palm adapter and articulated
grip geometry. Gameplay grip positions, IK reach and handedness stay unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** Three.js, TypeScript, Python/Pillow/numpy, local Blender, Node tests.

## Constraints
- No external generation or uploads.
- Preserve clothing, hair identity, skin tone, court, score and contact semantics.
- Female/male and canonical/left-reflected checks are separate.
- Do not claim full finger animation or recovered motion capture.
- Preserve before assets; preview has before/after and close cameras.
